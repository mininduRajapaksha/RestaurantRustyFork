A kitchen built on patience, fire, and family recipes

Rusty Fork began as a single wood-fired grill and a handwritten menu in a small corner of Colombo. What started as a weekend passion project between two friends who loved to cook has grown into a restaurant guests return to again and again.

Fifteen years on, the fire is still the heart of the kitchen - every dish passes through it, from the first sear to the final char. We work with growers within a two-hour radius and let the seasons, not the calendar, decide the menu.
